Questions

Can you take over a platform from another vendor?

Yes. On CEAT Specialty I took over a live multi-market AEM 6.5 platform with zero downtime and ran knowledge transfer with a risk tracker and a queries register.

Have you designed custom solutions?

Yes. For Royal Arc I was solution architect for a WordPress plugin that makes more than 50,000 PDFs searchable and usable across the site.

Do you set up support after launch?

Yes. On CEAT Specialty I designed and ran the managed-support model across eight workstreams. On Liquid I.V. support runs as sprints in Zoho Sprints.
