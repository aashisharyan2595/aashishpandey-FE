01How to use

How the tools work

  1. Step 1

    Pick a tool

    Notepad, URL shortener, image resizer, QR code generator and checker, lorem ipsum or launch checklist.

  2. Step 2

    Use it in your browser

    No sign-up and nothing to install.

  3. Step 3

    Take the result

    Download, copy or share what you made.

Small free tools I built for my own client work: a notepad, a URL shortener, an image resizer, a QR code generator and checker, a lorem ipsum generator and launch checklists. All are free and need no sign-up.

The notepad, image resizer, lorem generator and launch checklist run in your browser and keep what you type or upload on your device. The URL shortener is the exception, because it has to store the link you shorten on the server. The site counts page visits and button clicks with Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics, but it does not send the text or images you work on.

An llms.txt generator and a project estimate calculator are planned.

02FAQ

Free online tools: questions and answers

Are the tools free?

Yes. All of them are free and none needs an account.

Do you store my data?

The notepad, image resizer, lorem generator and checklist keep everything on your device. The URL shortener stores the link you give it, its short code and a click count until the link expires.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every tool runs in your web browser on desktop or mobile.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. Each tool works on phones and tablets as well as on a computer.

Can I print the QR codes on packaging or posters?

Yes. The QR code generator makes static codes with no expiry, no tracking and no watermark, so they are safe to print.

Can you build a tool for my team?

Yes. I scope, design and ship internal tools, sites and stores. Send a short brief and I will reply within 48 hours.
