Case study 01Liquid I.V. · Unilever

Liquid I.V. Shopify rollout across 15 markets

A global Shopify rollout across 15 markets: standalone stores for Canada, Australia, Mexico and Brazil, plus a shared-backend EU cluster. Nine new EU markets went live in H1 2026 while BAU ran on the markets already in production.

Client
Unilever
My role
Project Manager, Langoor
Timeline
Dec 2025 – present
Platform
Shopify multi-store
ProvesProject managementSEO
15markets in scope
12+live on platform
9new EU markets, H1 2026
On this page SummaryThe challengeMy roleWhat I didResultsMarketsStack and toolsQuestions

01Summary

Challenge
The brief covered 15 markets. Some were already trading and needed day-to-day support, while nine new EU markets still had to launch in the first half of 2026. The plan had to add markets without disrupting the stores already in production.
Approach
I split the rollout into two models: standalone regional stores for Canada, Australia, Mexico and Brazil, and a shared-backend EU cluster on liquid-iv.eu subdomains. Launches ran in parallel with BAU, which moved onto structured sprints in Zoho Sprints.
Outcome
Nine new EU markets went live in H1 2026 while support carried on for the live ones. Australia sessions rose 154%, Canada sessions 69% half on half, Mexico’s new-user audience grew 4×, and AI-driven mentions rose 1,490% month on month.
