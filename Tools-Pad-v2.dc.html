Online notepad with Markdown

This is a free online notepad that saves as you type. Write in plain text or Markdown, check the result in a live preview, and download the note as .txt or .md. There is no account and no sign-up.

What it does

  • Three views: Write, Split and Preview.
  • Autosave in your browser, with a word count, character count and reading time.
  • Several notes, with search, plus a dark mode and a focus mode (press Esc to leave it).
  • Export as plain text (.txt) or Markdown (.md).
  • Read-only share links.

Your notes stay on this device. They are saved in your browser’s local storage and nothing is sent to a server. Clear your browser data and the notes are gone, so download anything you want to keep.

Share makes a read-only link. The note is packed into the link itself, after the # sign, so it never touches a server. Anyone with the link can read it and save their own copy, so keep private notes out of it.

Questions

Is the notepad free?

Yes. It is free and there is no sign-up.

Where are my notes saved?

In your browser on this device only. Clearing site data deletes them.

Can I write in Markdown?

Yes. Use Split or Preview to see the formatted result as you type.

How does sharing work?

Share creates a read-only link that contains the note. Anyone with the link can read it. Search engines are told not to index shared notes.
