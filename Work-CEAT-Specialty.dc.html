Case study 04CEAT Specialty · CEAT Ltd

CEAT Specialty AEM vendor transition, zero downtime

I took over a live AEM platform that serves several markets, from the vendor who was leaving. The site never went down. Then I designed the support model and ran it across eight workstreams.

Client
CEAT Ltd
My role
Project Manager (Delivery Lead), Langoor
Timeline
May 2026 – present
Platform
Adobe Experience Manager 6.5
ProvesProject managementDevelopmentSEO
Zerodowntime at handover
47items in ~4 months
8workstreams
On this page SummaryThe challengeMy roleWhat I didResultsStack and toolsQuestions

Summary

Challenge
CEAT Specialty runs a live, multi-market enterprise AEM platform. It had to move from an outgoing vendor to a new team without any downtime, and then keep being maintained, enhanced and supported.
Approach
First came knowledge transfer. I kept a risk tracker, a queries register and regular status reviews. After that I set up BAU (business as usual) in sprints, with daily stand-ups and a weekly governance meeting with the client. The work was wide: new market pages, dispatcher configuration, CI/CD on Adobe Cloud Manager, AWS, QA, P1 incidents with root cause analysis, Core Web Vitals and technical SEO.
Outcome
The handover completed with zero downtime. In about four months the team delivered 47 items across eight workstreams. Along the way: 26 knowledge-transfer sessions, 35 tracked risks, 28 access items and around 107 governance touchpoints.

The challenge

CEAT Specialty runs a live, multi-market enterprise AEM platform. It had to move from an outgoing vendor to a new team without any downtime, and then keep being maintained, enhanced and supported.

My role

Project Manager (Delivery Lead), Langoor.

May 2026 – present

What I did

First came knowledge transfer. I kept a risk tracker, a queries register and regular status reviews. After that I set up BAU (business as usual) in sprints, with daily stand-ups and a weekly governance meeting with the client. The work was wide: new market pages, dispatcher configuration, CI/CD on Adobe Cloud Manager, AWS, QA, P1 incidents with root cause analysis, Core Web Vitals and technical SEO.

  1. 01Ran knowledge transfer with a risk tracker, a queries register and status governance
  2. 02Stood up sprint-based BAU: daily stand-ups, weekly client governance, Zoho Sprints
  3. 03Delivered new market pages for Ukraine, Canada, Brazil and more
  4. 04Led dispatcher config, CI/CD on Adobe Cloud Manager and AWS infrastructure (Route 53, ELB, RDS, S3)
  5. 05Ran QA cycles, P1 incidents with RCA, Adobe cases, Core Web Vitals and technical SEO

Results

The handover completed with zero downtime. In about four months the team delivered 47 items across eight workstreams. Along the way: 26 knowledge-transfer sessions, 35 tracked risks, 28 access items and around 107 governance touchpoints.

Project Manager (Delivery Lead) at Langoor (an LS Digital company).

Was there downtime during the vendor transition?

No. The live AEM platform changed vendors with zero downtime.

What stack does the platform use?

AEM 6.5, Adobe Cloud Manager, Adobe Commerce, AWS (Route 53, ELB, RDS, S3), a CDN and Google Search Console, managed in Zoho Sprints with Jira and Confluence.

What was delivered after the handover?

47 items in about four months across eight workstreams, including new market pages for Ukraine, Canada, Brazil and more.
