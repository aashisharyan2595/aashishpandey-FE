ATS-friendly resume templates

Every template here is built from real text in a normal reading order, with no images, tables or text boxes. That is what an applicant tracking system needs. The differences between them are about taste and fit, so choose the one that suits your field, then change the font, colours and spacing.

Classic resume template

One column, ruled headings, black text. The default for most applications.

Good for: Corporate, finance, operations, engineering and anyone unsure which to pick.

ATS readability: Highest

Modern resume template

One column with a colour accent on the name and headings. The text underneath is plain, so a parser reads it the same way as Classic.

Good for: Product, marketing, startups and tech roles.

ATS readability: High

Compact resume template

Smaller type and tighter spacing to keep a long career on one page.

Good for: Experienced people with many roles, and anyone close to spilling onto a second page.

ATS readability: High

Executive resume template

Serif type with a centred header and a calm layout.

Good for: Senior, management and leadership roles.

ATS readability: High

Minimal resume template

Plain headings with no rules or boxes and generous space.

Good for: Designers, writers, analysts and early-career applicants.

Sidebar resume template

Skills and certifications in a narrow side column. Looks distinctive, but some systems read two columns in the wrong order.

How applicant tracking systems read a resume

What an ATS actually does

An applicant tracking system is the software a company uses to collect applications. When you upload a file, it pulls out your name, contact details, jobs, dates, education and skills and stores them as fields. Recruiters then search and filter those fields, and a person decides who to call. You may read that most resumes are thrown out by robots. In most systems the software sorts and searches, and people make the decision, so the goal is a file that the software reads correctly and a person can skim in seconds.

Many employers use products such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS or Taleo. They differ in the details, but they share the same weak spots.

What trips a parser

  • Tables, text boxes and columns. Some systems read them out of order and mix your jobs with your skills.
  • Information in the header or footer. Contact details placed there are sometimes skipped. Keep them in the body of the page.
  • Images, icons and graphs. A skills bar or a logo is invisible to a parser, and a photo adds nothing.
  • Unusual headings. “My journey” will not be recognised as work history. Use Experience, Education, Skills and Projects.
  • Scanned pages. A picture of a page has no text. Export a real PDF or Word file.
  • Odd fonts and symbols. Stay with common fonts and plain bullets.

Formatting that works

  • One column, with your name and contact details at the top.
  • Standard section headings in a sensible order, newest job first.
  • Dates on every job, written the same way, such as “Mar 2022 - Present”.
  • Real bullets, one or two lines each.
  • One or two pages. One page suits most people with under about ten years of experience.

Use the words in the job description

Recruiters often search for a skill or a job title. If the posting says “stakeholder management” and your resume says “working with clients”, a search for the first phrase will not find you. Paste the job description into the match tool on this page. It lists the main words from the posting and shows which ones your resume already uses. Add the missing ones only where they are true, in your skills or in a bullet that shows them. Repeating words that have nothing to do with your work does not help, and a recruiter will notice it.

PDF or Word?

Most systems read both. Follow the posting if it names a format. Otherwise a PDF keeps your layout fixed, and a Word file is the safer choice when the form asks for a document it can edit. The PDF from this page contains real, selectable text, and you can also download a Word file or copy the plain text to paste into an application form.

How to write each section

Summary

Two or three lines: your job title, years of experience, the area you are strongest in, and one result. Leave out “hard-working team player” and write what you have done.

Experience

Start each bullet with a verb, say what you did, then say what changed. “Cut checkout load time from 4.1 s to 2.6 s by caching product pages” beats “Responsible for site speed”. Use numbers where you honestly can: percentages, money, time, team size. Put the strongest bullets first and trim jobs older than about ten to fifteen years.

Skills

Group them so they are easy to scan, such as Languages, Frameworks and Tools. List the tools and skills that appear in the posting and that you can talk about in an interview.

Education and certifications

Degree, school, location and years. Add a grade only if it helps you. Put certifications in their own section with the issuer and date.

Projects

Useful for engineers, designers, analysts and graduates. Give the name, a link if there is one, the tools and one or two lines on what you built and what it achieved.

02FAQ

Resume Maker: questions and answers

Is this resume maker really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how many resumes you make or how often you download them.

Is my resume stored on a server?

No. The page runs in your browser, and what you type is saved only in your browser’s own storage on your device. The PDF, Word file and plain text are made on your device too. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

A single column, real text, standard section headings, dates on every job, and no tables, text boxes, images or contact details hidden in the header. The templates here follow those rules. Matching the wording of the job description helps people find you when they search.

Which template is best for ATS?

Classic and Minimal are the safest, since their layout is the plainest. Modern, Compact and Executive are also single column and read the same way. Sidebar uses two columns, and some systems read those in the wrong order, so use it for direct applications rather than large job portals.

Should I save my resume as a PDF or a Word file?

Follow the instructions in the job posting. If there are none, both are usually accepted. The PDF from this page has selectable text, and you can also download a Word file or copy the plain text.

Can I add and remove sections?

Yes. Add Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Awards, Languages, Volunteering, Publications or a custom section. You can rename, reorder, hide or delete any of them.

Can I make a different resume for each job?

Yes, and it is worth doing. Change the summary and skills to match the posting, use the job match tool to see which words you are missing, and save each version as a file with the button above the preview.

How long should a resume be?

One page for most people with under about ten years of experience, and two pages for longer careers. Recruiters read quickly, so put the strongest points first.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

In the US, UK, Canada and for most corporate roles in India, leave it off. Conventions differ elsewhere, and a photo adds nothing for a parser. This maker has no photo field on purpose.

How far back should my work history go?

Ten to fifteen years is usual. Older roles can be shortened to a line or left out if they are not relevant.

What is the difference between a resume and a CV?

In the US and Canada a resume is a short, targeted summary of one to two pages, and a CV is a long academic document. In the UK, India and much of Europe, the word CV is used for what Americans call a resume.

Does the job match tool work like a real ATS?

No. It compares the main words in the job description with the words in your resume and shows the gaps. It is a guide for tailoring your wording, not a score from any employer’s system.

Will my resume be saved if I come back later?

Yes, in the same browser on the same device, until you clear your site data. To move it to another device, use Save as file and open the file there.

Does it work on a phone?

You can edit on a phone. Saving a PDF is more dependable from a computer, and on a phone you can download the Word file or copy the plain text instead.
