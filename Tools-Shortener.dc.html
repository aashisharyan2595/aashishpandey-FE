01How to use

How to shorten a link

    Any http or https address.

Paste a long link and get a short one on aashishpandey.com/s/. You can pick your own alias, choose when the link expires, and download a QR code as PNG or SVG. No sign-up.

How it works

  • Custom alias, optional: 3 to 32 letters, numbers, - or _.
  • Expiry: 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or 1 year. The default is 30 days.
  • QR code for every link, as PNG or SVG.
  • A list of your links, saved in your browser only.

To keep the service clean, each visitor can make 5 links an hour and 20 a day. Only http and https links work. Links to other shorteners, IP addresses, local hosts and known phishing patterns are blocked. Expired links stop redirecting.

The server stores the link you shorten, its short code and a click count until the link expires.

02FAQ

URL shortener: questions and answers

Is the URL shortener free?

Yes. It is free and needs no sign-up.

Do the short links expire?

Yes. You choose from 1 day to 1 year when you make the link, and it stops working after that. The default is 30 days.

Can I choose my own short link?

Yes. Add a custom alias of 3 to 32 letters, numbers, - or _. If someone already took it, pick another.

Can I get a QR code for my short link?

Yes. Every short link comes with a QR code you can download as PNG or SVG.

Which links are blocked?

Other link shorteners, IP addresses, local hosts, links with a username or password, and links that match known phishing patterns.

How many links can I make?

Each visitor can make 5 links an hour and 20 a day. The limit keeps the service clean for everyone.
