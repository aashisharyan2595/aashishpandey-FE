Connect Search Console

Shows your Google search numbers here, refreshed every 6 hours. It needs a read-only Google service account, once.

  1. In Google Cloud, create a project, turn on the Google Search Console API, and create a service account. Add a JSON key and download it.
  2. In Search Console, open Settings, Users and permissions, and add the service account's email (it ends in iam.gserviceaccount.com) as a Restricted user.
  3. In Vercel, add GSC_CLIENT_EMAIL (client_email from the JSON) and GSC_PRIVATE_KEY (private_key from the JSON, as it is), then redeploy. The property read is ; set GSC_SITE to change it.
