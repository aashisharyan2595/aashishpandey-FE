What I do

The work, and the proof.

01 · What I do

Multi-market Shopify rollouts

For Liquid I.V. (Unilever) I ran a Shopify rollout across 15 markets. Canada, Australia, Mexico and Brazil got standalone stores. The EU runs as a shared-backend cluster on liquid-iv.eu subdomains.

Nine new EU markets went live in H1 2026 while support carried on for the markets already live. I also handled analytics, hreflang for the UK, India and USA, and GEO work for AI search.

Read the Liquid I.V. case study
02 · What I do

Stores I built myself

Copper Chocs is a Shopify storefront with online cake ordering, same-day delivery and store pickup. Barùkas is a Shopify store with a product collection, a comparison with other nuts and recipe content.

On both I managed the project, built the store, ran the SEO, launched it and handled BAU support afterwards.

See the client sites
03 · What I do

SEO, launch and support

I run the SEO on the stores I build. On Liquid I.V. that covered GA4, Search Console and hreflang across markets.

After launch I stay on for BAU support. On Liquid I.V. that runs as sprints in Zoho Sprints, with a team of 20.

The route

How it works

  1. Step 01

    Tell me what you need

    A short brief is enough, or book a call and we talk it through. I want the markets, the date, and what is broken.

  2. Step 02

    You get a quote

    Scope, timeline and price, in writing, before I touch anything.

  3. Step 03

    I build it

    Theme, markets, apps, tracking. Every week you get a status note. Two minutes to read, no jargon.

  4. Step 04

    We test and launch

    I check it on real phones, in each market's currency and language, and in Search Console. Then we go live.

  5. Step 05

    Handover

    Your team gets notes and a walk-through. Want me around after launch? We can agree that up front.

7 answers

Questions

Can you run a multi-market Shopify rollout?

Yes. For Liquid I.V. (Unilever) I ran a rollout across 15 markets: standalone stores for Canada, Australia, Mexico and Brazil, plus a shared-backend EU cluster. Nine new EU markets went live in H1 2026.

Do you build Shopify stores from scratch?

Yes. I built the Copper Chocs and Barùkas stores. On both I managed the project, built the store, ran the SEO, launched it and handled BAU support afterwards.

Do you handle SEO for Shopify stores?

Yes. On Liquid I.V. that covered GA4, Search Console and hreflang for the UK, India and USA, plus GEO work for AI search.

How do I start a Shopify project with you?

Email hello@aashishpandey.com, book a 20-minute call, or send a short brief. I reply within 48 hours.

What should I send for a quote?

The store link, what you want done, which countries you sell in and the launch date. A rough budget helps, but skip it if you do not have one.

Have you done multi-market stores?

Yes. Liquid I.V. was 15 markets on one Shopify platform. I did the analytics and the hreflang for the UK, India and USA.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the work, so I will not guess here. You get a number in writing after the brief.

Available for full-time and freelance

Working together.

Freelance: send a short brief and I will come back with scope, timeline and a quote. Full-time: I am open to Program Manager roles. Either way, the fastest start is a 20-minute call.
