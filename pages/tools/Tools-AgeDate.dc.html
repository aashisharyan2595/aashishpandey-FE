Work out your age as on any date, or the days between two dates, in one place.

An age calculator that gives your exact age in years, months and days on any date you choose, checks an exam age limit, counts the days between two dates, and adds or subtracts days, weeks, months and years. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Also called age calculator, age as on date calculator, date difference calculator, days between dates calculator or date add subtract calculator.

  • Nothing is sent anywhere
  • Exam age-limit check

Exam age limit check (optional)
Upper limit means

“Up to” allows someone who turns exactly the maximum age on the cut-off date. “Below” does not. Use the wording in your notification.

Operation

Weeks count as 5 working days, and days are working days. Years and months are added first. Public holidays are not skipped.

How to use the age and date calculator

  1. 1

    Pick what you need

    Choose Age, Days between, or Add or subtract. Each keeps its own dates, so you can switch without losing anything.

  2. 2

    Enter the dates

    Use the date boxes, or tap Today. For an exam form, set the “Age as on” date to the cut-off in the notification.

  3. 3

    Read and copy the answer

    The result updates as you type. Copy it as plain text to paste into a form, a message or a document.

Most forms do not ask how old you are today. They ask how old you were on a fixed date, such as the closing date of an application or 1 August of the exam year. This calculator works out your age on that exact date, in completed years, months and days, which is how exam notifications and many official forms count it.

The other two tabs cover everyday date sums: how many days remain until a deadline, how long ago something happened, or which date falls 90 days after a given day. Dates are calculated in your browser using calendar arithmetic, so daylight-saving changes cannot shift an answer by a day.

A note on exam age limits

Rules differ between notifications. Some say candidates must be “not more than 27 years” and some say “below 27 years”, and the two treat someone who turns exactly 27 on the cut-off date differently. The age-limit check lets you choose either reading and add category relaxation, but the official notification always decides.

What it does

  • Exact age in years, months and days as on today or any cut-off date, such as 1 August 2026
  • Exam age-limit check: enter the minimum, maximum and any relaxation to see if you qualify and the birth-date window that does
  • Total age in months, weeks, days and hours, the weekday you were born and a countdown to your next birthday
  • Days between two dates, with the years-months-days split, weeks, and working days (Monday to Friday)
  • Add or subtract years, months, weeks and days from a date, with an optional working-days mode that skips weekends
  • Leap-day (29 February) birthdays and month-end dates are handled and explained

Age & Date Calculator: questions and answers

How do I calculate my age as on a particular date?

Enter your date of birth, then set the “Age as on” box to the date you need, for example the cut-off date in an exam notification. The tool counts the complete years, then the remaining months and days, so 15 August 1995 as on 1 August 2026 is 30 years, 11 months and 17 days.

How is age counted for SSC, UPSC, bank and other government exams?

Notifications give a cut-off date and an age range, and age is counted in completed years as on that date. Whether the upper limit includes someone who turns that age exactly on the cut-off date differs between notifications, and categories such as OBC, SC, ST and ex-servicemen get extra years. Use the exam age limit section here to test both readings, and confirm against the official notice.

How many days are between two dates?

Use the Days between tab. By default the end date is not counted, so 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. Tick “Count the end date too” if you want both days included, which is how many deadlines and leave applications are counted. It also shows the weeks, the years-months-days split and the working days.

How do I find the date 90 days from today?

Open the Add or subtract tab, leave the start on today, enter 90 in the days box and the answer appears with its weekday. Use the presets for 30, 60 and 90 days, or switch on working days to skip Saturdays and Sundays. Public holidays are not included, because they differ by state and year.

What happens if I was born on 29 February?

In years that are not leap years, this tool treats your birthday as 28 February for the next-birthday countdown. Some forms and legal contexts use 1 March instead, so check which one applies. Your age in years, months and days is not affected.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

No. The page does not upload or save what you type. All the arithmetic runs in your browser, and nothing is kept once you close or refresh the page.
