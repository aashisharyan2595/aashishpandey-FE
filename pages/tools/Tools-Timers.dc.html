A free Pomodoro timer, countdown timer, stopwatch with laps and a sectioned exam timer on one page. They run on your device clock, so switching tabs does not slow them, and a refresh does not lose them. No ads and nothing to install.

    How to use the online timers

    1. 1

      Choose a timer

      Pick Pomodoro, Countdown, Stopwatch or Exam. Each has its own time, so you can run more than one.

    2. 2

      Set it and press Start

      Change the minutes or tap a preset. Press Space to start, or use the button. Sound is allowed once you press Start.

    3. 3

      Keep working

      The time shows in this tab’s title. When time is up you get a sound, a message here and, if you allowed it, a notification.

    Most online timers count with a ticking interval. When you switch tabs, the browser slows those ticks, and the timer falls behind. These timers store the moment they should finish and compare it with the device clock on every update, so the answer is right whenever you look, even after a laptop sleeps for a while.

    The Pomodoro technique alternates focused work with short breaks, usually 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of rest, with a longer break after four rounds. The exam timer applies the same idea to a paper with parts: you list the sections and minutes, and it moves from one to the next and warns you before each one ends.

    What is kept on your device

    Your settings, the running state and the day’s Pomodoro count are saved in this browser only, so a refresh does not lose them. Nothing is sent to a server. Use Reset on any timer to clear it.

    What it does

    • Pomodoro timer with focus, short break and long break lengths you set, an optional auto-start, and a count of the focus sessions you finished today
    • Countdown timer from hours, minutes and seconds, quick presets, or “count down to a clock time” such as 5:30 PM
    • Stopwatch with laps, split times, best and worst laps highlighted, and a copy button
    • Exam timer with named sections, a chime when each ends, the next section starting by itself, and warnings at 30, 15, 10, 5 or 1 minute left
    • Timing uses the device clock rather than counting ticks, so a throttled background tab does not drift
    • Full-screen display, a sound alarm made in the browser, an optional desktop notification, and the screen kept awake while a timer runs
    • Shows the time in the browser tab title and remembers running timers if you refresh by mistake
    • Keyboard shortcuts: Space to start or pause, L for a lap, R to reset and F for full screen

    Online Timers: questions and answers

    Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs or lock my screen?

    Yes for tabs. It compares the device clock with the finish time instead of counting ticks, so it stays accurate in a background tab. A phone or laptop that goes to sleep may not play the alarm on time because the browser is paused, but the time shown is correct as soon as you wake it. Leave “Keep the screen awake” on to avoid sleep while a timer runs.

    How does the Pomodoro timer work?

    It runs a focus period, then a short break, and after the number of rounds you choose, a long break. The defaults are 25, 5 and 15 minutes with a long break after four rounds, and you can change all of them. Tick auto-start to move to the next period by itself. The tool counts the focus sessions you complete today.

    How do I use the exam timer?

    List each section with its minutes, or pick a preset, then press Start. At the end of a section it chimes and starts the next one, and it warns you at the minutes-left marks you switch on. Use it for mock tests and practice papers, and keep the official clock as the real one in an actual exam.

    Can I set a timer to a clock time, such as 5:30 PM?

    Yes. In Countdown, enter the time under “Count down to a clock time” and press Start until. If that time has already passed today, it counts down to the same time tomorrow.

    Why is there no sound or notification?

    Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page, which pressing Start counts as. Check that the sound box is ticked and your device is not muted. For a desktop notification, tick the notification box and allow it when the browser asks; it only appears when this tab is in the background.

    Is anything uploaded or tracked?

    No. The timers run entirely in your browser, and the settings stay in this browser’s storage.
