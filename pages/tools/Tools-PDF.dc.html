      How to merge, split or sign a PDF

      1. 1

        Add your files

        Drop PDFs (and photos if you like) on the box, or tap to choose. Nothing is uploaded: the files are opened in this tab.

      2. 2

        Arrange or place

        Drag cards into order, tick the pages to keep, rotate them, or press Place signature and tap the spot on the page.

      3. 3

        Download

        The finished file is built on your device and downloads straight away. Split into many files and you get one ZIP.

      Most free PDF sites make you upload your contract, ID or invoice to their server, then limit you to a couple of jobs a day, cap the file size, stamp a watermark on the result or ask for a card to continue. This tool does the work inside your browser instead. The page contains its own small PDF engine, so a merge, a split or a signature is just your device rearranging the file you gave it. That is why it has no limits and why your document stays private.

      What it does

      • Merge any number of PDFs, and turn JPG, PNG or WebP photos into pages.
      • Reorder by dragging, or with the arrow buttons on each card. Type a range such as 1-3, 7 to use only some pages of a file.
      • See every page as a thumbnail before you cut. Select by click, shift-click a run, odd or even pages, or type a range.
      • Download the selected pages, download the file without them, or split into single pages, every N pages or your own ranges.
      • Rotate any page, or the whole document, left or right.
      • Sign with a drawn, typed or uploaded signature. Photos of a signature get their white paper background removed.
      • Add initials, today’s date or a line of text. Drag to move, pull the corner to resize, or put an item on every page.
      • Optionally remember your signature on this device only. It is never sent anywhere, and Forget clears it.

      What a signature here is, and is not

      The signature you place is an image on the page, the same as signing a printout and scanning it. That is accepted for most everyday paperwork: approvals, quotes, rental and service agreements, school and office forms. It is not a certificate-based digital signature, so it has no cryptographic seal or audit trail. If a contract or a law asks for a certified signature, use a trust-service provider for that step.

      Password-protected PDFs are not supported: remove the password in the app that made the file first. Fillable form fields keep their look, but are not editable after a merge.

      PDF merge, split and sign: questions and answers

      How do I merge PDF files without uploading them?

      Open the Merge tab, drop your PDFs (photos work too), put them in the order you want and press Merge. The new file is built inside your browser tab and downloaded from there, so nothing is sent to a server. You can type page ranges like 1-3, 7 on a card to use only some pages of a file.

      Can I pick or remove pages from a PDF?

      Yes. In the Split tab you see a thumbnail of every page. Click pages to select them, or type a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-. Then download only those pages, download the file without them, or split the whole document into one file per page, every N pages or custom ranges. Rotation is available too.

      How do I sign a PDF for free?

      Open the Sign tab, drop the PDF, then draw your signature with a mouse, finger or pen, type your name in a handwriting style, or upload a photo of your signature. Press Place signature, tap where it should go, drag or resize it, add a date or initials, then Apply and download.

      Is a signature added here legally binding?

      It is an electronic signature: an image of your signature placed on the page, which is what most everyday agreements, forms and approvals need. It is not a certificate-based digital signature, so it does not carry a cryptographic seal or an audit trail. Where a law or a contract asks for a certified signature, use a trust-service provider for that.

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. The tool is a web page that runs in your browser. Your PDFs are read and written in your device's memory and are never sent to a server, which is why there are no file-size caps, daily limits, accounts or watermarks. Turn off your network after the page loads and it still works.

      Why can't it open my PDF?

      The most common reason is a password: password-protected PDFs are not supported, so remove the password in the app that made the file and try again. A damaged or scanned-only file can also fail. Large files work, but a very big PDF needs enough free memory on your device.
