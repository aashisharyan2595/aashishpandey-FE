Plan and track a project on one board.

Drag cards across a Kanban board, switch to a list or a timeline, and keep owners, dates and checklists in one place. It runs in your browser and saves as you type.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Board, list and timeline
  • Saved in your browser

Your board is saved in this browser only. Export it as a file to back it up or to share it with a teammate.

How to run a project on the board

  1. 1

    Start from a template or a blank board

    Pick website launch, software sprint or marketing campaign, or start empty and name your own columns.

  2. 2

    Add cards and fill in the details

    Give each card an owner, a priority, start and due dates, labels and a checklist.

  3. 3

    Move work along and read the plan

    Drag cards between columns, filter by person or label, and switch to the list or the timeline to see dates.

Most small projects do not need a heavy tool. They need one place that shows what is waiting, what is being worked on and what is done, with a date and an owner on every task. This board does that without an account: it opens straight to a working template, keeps everything in your browser and lets you take the plan with you as a file.

What it does

  • Kanban board with draggable cards and columns you can rename, recolour, add and remove. The last column counts as done.
  • Cards with an assignee, priority, labels, start and due dates, notes and a checklist with progress.
  • List view you can sort by any column, and a timeline that draws each dated card as a bar with a line for today.
  • Search and filters by assignee, priority and label, plus a hide-done switch.
  • Overdue and due-soon dates are marked, and a progress bar shows how much of the board is done.
  • Export to a spreadsheet (CSV), a board file you can open again, or copy the board as plain text. Print to save a PDF.

Project Management Board: questions and answers

Is the project management tool free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no card and no limit on cards or boards you keep in your browser.

Where is my board saved?

In this browser, using local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing site data or using another browser or device starts you with an empty board, so export a file now and then as a backup.

Can my team use the same board?

There is no live sharing or accounts. Export the board as a file and send it, and a teammate can open it on their own device. For work that needs live edits by several people, a hosted tool is a better fit.

How does the timeline view work?

Every card with a start date, a due date or both appears as a bar. Bars take the colour of their column, a line marks today, and overdue cards get a red outline. Cards without dates are counted but not drawn.

Can I move cards on a phone?

Yes. Press and hold a card for a moment, then drag it to another column. You can also open the card and change its status from the menu.

How is this different from Trello or Jira?

It is smaller on purpose: one board, no accounts, no integrations. It suits one person or a small team planning a website, a sprint or a campaign, and it keeps your data on your device.
