A whiteboard for quick ideas.

Sketch, map out a plan or run a brainstorm on an endless canvas. Draw with a pen, add shapes, arrows, sticky notes and images, then save it as a picture. No sign-up, and it works on a phone too.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Endless canvas
  • Saved in your browser

The board is saved in this browser only. Save it as a PNG or a board file to keep or share it.

How to use the whiteboard

  1. 1

    Pick a tool

    Choose the pen, a shape, text or a sticky note from the toolbar. Press V for select, P for pen, R for a rectangle.

  2. 2

    Draw, add notes and move around

    Drag to draw. Scroll or use the Pan tool to move, and hold Ctrl and scroll or pinch to zoom. Paste or drop an image to add it.

  3. 3

    Save or share the board

    Download a PNG for a slide or a chat, or save a board file you can open here again. The board also autosaves in this browser.

A whiteboard is the fastest way to think out loud: a rough flow, a layout, a list of ideas on sticky notes. This one opens instantly, never asks you to sign in and keeps the canvas endless so you do not run out of room. Everything stays in your browser.

What it does

  • Pen, highlighter and an eraser that removes whole strokes.
  • Rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, lines and arrows that you can move, resize and fill.
  • Text and sticky notes you edit by double-clicking, with adjustable size and colour.
  • Paste, drop or upload images and place them anywhere on the board.
  • Pan and zoom with the mouse, trackpad, keyboard or two fingers on a touch screen.
  • Undo and redo, duplicate, bring to front, a dot grid and a light or dark board.
  • Export as a PNG (with or without a background) or save the whole board as a file.

Online Whiteboard: questions and answers

Is the online whiteboard free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how much you draw.

Is my whiteboard saved?

Yes, automatically, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. If you clear site data or switch device the board will not be there, so save a PNG or a board file for anything you want to keep.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Draw with a finger or a stylus, pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to move. The toolbar scrolls sideways on a small screen.

Can several people draw on the same board?

Not live. This is a private, single-user board. To share it, export a PNG or send the board file and the other person can open it on their device.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

V select, H pan, P pen, M highlighter, E eraser, R rectangle, O ellipse, D diamond, L line, A arrow, T text, N sticky note, I image. Ctrl or Cmd with Z undoes, with D duplicates, with A selects all. Hold Space to pan, and press Shift and 1 to fit everything on screen.

How do I export my drawing?

Open the Board menu and choose Save as PNG, PNG transparent or Save board file. The PNG covers everything on the board, not just the visible part.
