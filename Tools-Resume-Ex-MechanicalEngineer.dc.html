Resume example

Mechanical engineer resume example

Software, standards, licences, projects. The sample on the left uses made-up details. Replace them with yours.

What makes it work

  • List software and standards first, such as CAD, FEA and ASME.
  • Describe projects with size, load, cost or tolerance.
  • Show licences and registrations clearly with the year.
  • Say what changed: cost, weight, time or failure rate.

Skills to show

SolidWorksAutoCADANSYS (FEA)CFD basicsGD&TASME Section VIIIB31.3API 650Design reviewsvendor coordinationcost estimatingroot-cause analysis

Bullets that read well

Designed 18 pressure vessels to ASME VIII, with no rework at third-party inspection.
Cut fabrication cost by 14% on a $22M project by changing weld and support details.
Ran FEA studies in ANSYS that removed 2 tonnes of steel from a skid design.

Leave out

  • Duties with no result.
  • Burying the licence at the bottom of page two.
Questions

Mechanical engineer resume FAQ

What should a mechanical engineer put on a resume?

Software and standards, projects with numbers, your degree and any licence or EIT status. Use the terms in the posting.

Should I list CAD software?

Yes, in Skills and again in the job where you used it, so a keyword search finds you either way.
