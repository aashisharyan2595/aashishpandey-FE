Animated emoji in this chat
===========================
Source: "Noto Emoji Animation" by Google (https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/)
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: each animation was downloaded once, resized to 96 x 96 pixels, reduced in frame rate and re-encoded as animated WebP so the page stays fast.
The files are stored in this project's GitHub repository and loaded through the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net), a free service with no sign-in. Nothing is loaded from Google while the chat runs.

Built-in animated stickers (the bouncing and shaking emoji in the Stickers tab) use the standard emoji of your own device and CSS animation written for this site.
