      01How to use

      How to find a meeting time across time zones

      1. Step 1

        Add the cities

        Type a city or a country and press Enter. The first city is yours, and the timeline starts from midnight there.

      2. Step 2

        Set the day and working hours

        Pick the date, since daylight saving changes things, and the hours people normally work. Early and late hours show in amber.

      3. Step 3

        Pick a time

        Click the timeline, drag the slider or use a suggestion. Then copy the times for everyone, share the link or download an invite.

      Booking a call across three or four time zones usually means opening a few clocks and doing sums. This planner lines every city up on one 24-hour timeline for the day you choose, shades working hours, early and late hours and night, and shows where they all overlap. Nothing is looked up online: it uses the time zone data already built into your browser.

      What it does

      • Eight cities at once, found by city, country or time zone name.
      • A green strip marks every half hour when all of them are inside working hours.
      • Suggests the three best start times, even when nothing fits perfectly.
      • Works for the date you pick, so daylight saving changes are right.
      • Copy the times for each city, copy a link that restores the same plan, or download an .ics calendar file.
      • Works offline once the page has loaded.
      02FAQ

      Time Zone Meeting Planner: questions and answers

      Does it handle daylight saving time?

      Yes. Times are worked out for the date you pick using your browser’s built-in time zone data, so a meeting after the clocks change shows the right offsets. Change the date to see how the gap between two cities moves.

      Why does India show a half-hour offset?

      India is UTC+5:30, and a few other places such as Nepal, parts of Australia and Iran also sit on 30 or 45 minute offsets. The timeline is built in half-hour steps, so these line up correctly.

      What counts as working hours?

      Whatever you set. The default is 9:00 to 18:00. The two hours either side are marked early or late, and anything beyond that is night. Suggestions try to avoid night times first, then early and late ones.

      Are my cities sent anywhere?

      No. Your choices are saved in your own browser and in the link you copy, which carries them in the part after the # sign, so they are not sent to a server when someone opens it.

      How do I send the plan to someone?

      Press Copy link. Whoever opens it sees the same cities, day, working hours and chosen time. You can also copy the times as text or download a calendar file that opens in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

      Does it need an internet connection?

      Only to load the page. After that it works offline, because the time zone data is part of the browser.
