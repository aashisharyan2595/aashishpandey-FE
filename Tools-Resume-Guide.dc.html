The ATS resume guide

What an applicant tracking system does, what trips it up and how to write a resume that works for it and for people.

What an ATS doesWhat breaks itFormattingKeywordsPDF or WordWriting bulletsFAQ

What an ATS does

It collects applications, pulls your details into fields, and lets recruiters search and filter. A person still decides who to call. So the goal is a file the software reads correctly and a person can skim in seconds.

Many employers use products such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS or Taleo. They differ, but they share the same weak spots.

What breaks it

Avoid

  • Tables, text boxes and columns
  • Contact details in the header or footer
  • Images, icons and skill bars
  • Creative headings such as “My journey”
  • Scanned pages with no real text

Do

  • One column with your name on top
  • Standard headings: Experience, Education, Skills
  • Dates on every job, written the same way
  • Real bullets, one or two lines each
  • A common font and plain symbols

Formatting rules

  • One or two pages. One page suits under about ten years of experience.
  • Newest job first, with a title, company and dates.
  • Group skills so they scan: languages, frameworks, tools.
  • No photo, date of birth or home address.

Use the words in the posting

Recruiters search for skills and job titles. If the posting says “stakeholder management” and your resume says “working with clients”, a search for the first will not find you.

Paste the job description into the builder’s Check step. It lists the main words from the posting and which ones you already use. Add the missing ones only where they are true.

PDF or Word?

PDF

Keeps your layout fixed. The PDF from this tool has real, selectable text.

Word

The safer choice when a form wants a document it can edit. You can also copy plain text.

Follow the posting if it names a format.

Questions

Is this resume maker really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, and no limit on resumes or downloads.

Is my resume stored on a server?

No. It runs in your browser and saves only on your device. The PDF, Word file and text are made on your device too.

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

A single column, real text, standard headings, dates on every job, and no tables, text boxes, images or details hidden in the header.

Which template is best for ATS?

Classic and Minimal are the safest. Modern, Compact and Executive are also single column. Sidebar uses two columns, which some systems misread.

Should I save a PDF or a Word file?

Follow the posting. If it does not say, both are usually fine. This tool gives you PDF, Word and plain text.

Can I add and remove sections?

Yes. Add, rename, reorder, hide or delete any section, including custom ones.

Can I make a different resume for each job?

Yes. Change the summary and skills for the posting, check the keyword match, and save each version as a file.

How long should a resume be?

One page for under about ten years of experience, two pages for longer careers.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

In the US, UK, Canada and most corporate roles in India, no. This maker has no photo field on purpose.

How far back should my work history go?

Ten to fifteen years is usual. Shorten or drop older roles that are not relevant.

What is the difference between a resume and a CV?

In the US and Canada a resume is short and targeted, and a CV is a long academic document. In the UK, India and much of Europe, CV means what Americans call a resume.

Does the job match tool work like a real ATS?

No. It compares the main words in a job posting with your resume and shows gaps. It is a guide, not a score from any employer system.

Will my resume be saved if I come back?

Yes, in the same browser on the same device, until you clear site data. Use Save as file to move it.

Does it work on a phone?

You can edit on a phone. Saving a PDF is more dependable from a computer, and the Word file or plain text work on a phone.
