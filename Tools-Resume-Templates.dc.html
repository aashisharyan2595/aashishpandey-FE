Choosing

Not sure which?

Applying through a job portal

Use Classic or Minimal. Their layout is the plainest, so the parser has the least to get wrong.

Sending it to a person

Banner, Timeline, Executive or Creative give a stronger look. Keep the content the same and check it with the ATS tool.
