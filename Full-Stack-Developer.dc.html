How it works

  1. 01

    Start with a message

    Send a few lines on what has to work and who uses it. A 20-minute call works too.

  2. 02

    Scope and quote

    I split the build into stages. You approve each one, so nothing big gets built on a guess.

  3. 03

    Build

    Code goes through CI/CD, so every change is tested before it reaches the live site.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Performance and security checks first. Then a launch with a way back if something breaks.

  5. 05

    Hand it over

    You get the access and the notes. I can stay on for support, or not. Up to you.

Questions

What kind of things do you build?

Marketing sites, custom CMS and eCommerce builds, and internal tools. Recent work includes Shopify stores, WordPress catalogue sites and a 3D portfolio built with Three.js.

Do you also manage the project?

Yes. On most of my client work I managed the project and built it. On the sites in my portfolio I managed, built, ran the SEO, launched and handled BAU support.

Are you open to full-time roles?

I am open to Program Manager roles and take freelance briefs alongside.

How do I send a brief?

Email hello@aashishpandey.com, book a 20-minute call, or send a short brief. I reply within 48 hours.

What can you build?

Custom CMS and ecommerce sites. WordPress, Shopify and Webflow builds too. Deployment runs through Docker and GitHub Actions.

Will you work with my developers?

Yes. I have worked inside existing teams, and I have taken over live platforms from other vendors.

What does it cost?

I quote after I have seen the brief. In writing, before work starts.

Working together

Freelance: send a short brief and I will come back with scope, timeline and a quote. Full-time: I am open to Program Manager roles. Either way, the fastest start is a 20-minute call.
