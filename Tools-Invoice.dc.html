01How to use

How to make an invoice

  1. Step 1

    Add your details

    Enter your business, your client and the invoice number. Add a logo if you have one. Your details are remembered for the next invoice.

  2. Step 2

    List what you are charging for

    Add each item with a quantity, a rate and a tax rate. Add a discount or a deposit if needed. Totals and tax work out as you type.

  3. Step 3

    Print or save as PDF

    Press Print or save as PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print window. You can also save the invoice as a file to edit later.

Most free invoice makers want an account, put their name across the page or keep your client list on their servers. This one does none of that. It is a form and a live preview, and the PDF comes from your browser’s own print function, so it works with any currency symbol and any language you type.

What it does

  • Your logo, business details, bank or UPI details and notes on every invoice.
  • Line items with quantity, rate and a tax rate for each, so GST or VAT at different rates can sit on one invoice.
  • Percentage or fixed discount, and an amount already paid, with the balance due shown.
  • Ten currencies, A4 or Letter paper and five accent colours.
  • Optional amount in words, in the Indian lakh and crore style for rupees.
  • Save an invoice as a file and open it later, or start the next one with your details already filled in.
02FAQ

Invoice Generator: questions and answers

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on the number of invoices and no watermark. A small “made with” footer is available but it is off unless you switch it on.

How do I get a PDF?

Press Print or save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the destination in the print window. The invoice is laid out for A4 or Letter paper, whichever you pick, and the invoice number is suggested as the file name.

Is my client and business data uploaded?

No. Everything stays in your browser. Your details are saved in your browser’s own storage so they are ready next time, and you can wipe them with Clear saved data. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I charge GST or VAT?

Yes. Give each line a tax percentage. Lines at the same rate are grouped, so an invoice with 18% and 5% items shows both tax amounts. Set the label to GST, VAT, Sales tax or anything else. Check the tax rules and invoice requirements for your country, since this tool does not know them.

How is the invoice number handled?

Type any format you like, such as INV-0042 or 2026/014. When you press New invoice the trailing number goes up by one, and your business details stay filled in.

Can I edit an invoice later?

Yes. Use Save invoice file to download it, and Open invoice file to load it back. The file is plain JSON, so keep it with your records.
